Important Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Do package prices vary based on the time slot? ▼
Yes! Package pricing depends on your selected time slot and included meals. For example, standard Lunch Day Out runs 10:00 AM – 05:30 PM, whereas Breakfast + Lunch starts at 09:00 AM, and Full Day Out runs till 10:00 PM with dinner included. You can view each slot rate directly on the package cards above.
What are the active weekday and weekend discounts? ▼
On weekdays (Monday to Thursday), you can avail Flat 20% OFF using coupon code: SAVE20NOW on Carnival and Extravagant packages. On weekends (Friday to Sunday), an exclusive 15% OFF is active on select packages!
What is the dress code for the pool and activities? ▼
Synthetic nylon or lycra swimwear is strictly compulsory to access the swimming pool. For rope challenges and adventure sports, comfortable sportswear and closed athletic shoes are mandatory.
What is the child policy? ▼
Children between 0 and 5 years are completely complimentary. Children aged 6 to 12 years are charged at discounted kid rates (₹600 on weekdays / ₹700 on weekends). Children aged 12 and above are considered adults.
